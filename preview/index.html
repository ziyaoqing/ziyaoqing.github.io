02 / About

Learning to connect
mechanism with medicine.

I am a Dean’s Scholar studying pharmaceutical science at Monash University. My interests span microglia, neuroinflammation, tau pathology and brain clearance. Research placements in Japan have introduced me to these questions at different scales, from proteins and organelles to blood-forming cells. I want to understand how changes outside the brain influence its vulnerability to dementia, and how that understanding could guide drug discovery. I am particularly interested in distinguishing peripheral signals from the brain’s own response, and in testing the links between them.
