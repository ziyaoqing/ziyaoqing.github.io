My name is Ziyao Qing. I am a Dean's Scholar in the Bachelor of Pharmaceutical Science Advanced (Honours) Scholars Program at the Monash Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences (MIPS), Monash University, Melbourne.

I want to understand why the ageing brain becomes vulnerable to dementia, and to help turn that understanding into new medicines. My interests sit between neuroscience and pharmacology: microglia and neuroinflammation, tau pathology, the brain's clearance systems and, more and more, the link between the brain and the bone marrow that produces circulating immune cells.

One question guides my current interests: how does the blood-forming system influence the ageing brain? I am interested in separating changes in circulating cells and signals from the brain’s own response, and in identifying which links could become therapeutic targets.

So far I have trained in four laboratories in Japan, each working on neurodegeneration from a different angle: the Alzheimer's-related kinase MARK4, inflammation in the ageing brain, contacts between organelles, and the role of blood stem cells in dementia. Each project, and what I am thinking about now, is described on the Research page.
